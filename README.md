# 💊 MedTrack

> **Stay on track, stay cared for.**

**MedTrack** is a native Android application designed to make medication management easier for **caregivers and patients**. It helps caregivers organize and monitor medication schedules for multiple patients, while providing patients with a simple way to view their daily medication schedule and receive reminders.

The goal of MedTrack is simple: **make medication routines easier to manage, easier to remember, and easier to share.**

---

## 🌟 About MedTrack

Managing medication can become challenging, especially when a caregiver is responsible for multiple patients with different medication schedules.

MedTrack provides a centralized solution where caregivers can manage patient information, organize medication schedules, monitor daily treatments, and collaborate with other caregivers.

For patients, MedTrack keeps things simple. Patients can access the schedules prepared by their caregiver and receive reminders without having to manage complicated medical information themselves.

---

## 👥 User Roles

MedTrack provides different experiences based on the user's role.

### 🧑‍⚕️ Caregiver

Caregivers have full access to patient and medication management features.

They can:

- Manage multiple patients
- Create and organize medication schedules
- Monitor daily medication schedules
- Send medication reminders
- Share patient information with connected caregivers
- Connect with other caregivers
- Manage profile and application settings

### 🧑 Patient

The patient interface is designed to be simple and easy to understand.

Patients can:

- Access MedTrack using a verification code provided by their caregiver
- View their daily medication schedule
- Receive medication reminders and notifications
- View connected caregiver information
- Access their profile information

---

## ✨ Main Features

### 📅 Medication Schedule
Keep daily medication schedules organized so caregivers can easily monitor upcoming medication times.

### 👥 Patient Management
Caregivers can manage multiple patients and maintain their individual information and medication schedules in one place.

### 🔔 Medication Reminder
Notifications help remind patients about their medication schedule, while caregivers can also send reminders when needed.

### 🤝 Sharing & Caregiver Connection
MedTrack allows caregivers to connect and share patient access, making collaboration easier when multiple caregivers are involved in a patient's care.

### 🔐 Role-Based Access
Caregivers and patients have different levels of access. Caregivers manage patient information and schedules, while patients receive a simplified view focused on their own medication routine.

### 🔑 Simple Patient Access
Instead of requiring a complicated registration process, patients can access MedTrack using a verification code generated and shared by their caregiver.

---

## 🎯 Our Goal

MedTrack aims to reduce missed medication schedules and simplify medication management, especially for elderly patients, people with disabilities, patients with long-term health conditions, and caregivers responsible for multiple patients.

By combining **medication scheduling, reminders, patient management, and caregiver collaboration**, MedTrack helps make everyday medication routines more organized and manageable.
